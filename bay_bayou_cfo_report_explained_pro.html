⚠️ 4. The Math Mystery: Why Monthly Net Income Didn't Add Up

In Jim’s report on Page 4, if you sum the monthly net income figures (Jan $331K + Feb $257K + Mar $176K + Apr $16K - May $60K - Jun $69K), they equal $652,397. But the total printed on the right says $385,621!

The Explanation: Jim’s Excel template had a formula glitch on the monthly columns. In each individual month, the formula calculated Net Income = NOI - Depreciation - Amortization + Interest Income, but accidentally left out Mortgage Interest Expense ($45K/month)! The 6-month difference ($652,397 - $385,621) is exactly $266,776—the exact Byline Bank mortgage interest for the period!
