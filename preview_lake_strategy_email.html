1. The 1/2 Installment Advantage ($5.12M Pool)

By parsing Cycle 1 vs. Cycle 2 dues from the county property portal, we identified that when Cycle 1 is $10 or less (statutory advertising fee) and Cycle 2 has the entire tax bill, the taxpayer or mortgage escrow already paid the first installment.

There are 268 parcels over $10,000 that are 2nd Installment Only ($5,119,825 total). Because the property is actively occupied and half the year is already paid, redemption probability is over 95%. This $5.12M pool is 2.5x larger than our $2.0M Lake goal, meaning we can hit our entire target on safe 1/2 installments alone.

2. The 4% Bidding Edge (Beating the Bridge Lottery)

At Kane, Bridge took 44% of the sale at 5%. If we bid 5% to match Bridge, the auction software runs a random tie-breaker, and Bridge's scale wins the volume.

Our Rule: Bid 4% on parcels >=$25,000.
On a $50k or $100k ticket, giving up 1% on the initial penalty is trivial ($500) because the real margin is made on the 12% statutory annual subtax carrying interest (+500 to +600 bps spread over Byline Bank borrowing cost). Bidding 4% guarantees we win the ticket over Bridge every time.
